PROJECT CASE STUDY

Room Renovation and 5 Year Design Review

3D Modeling, Room Layout, Interior Design, Real life Measurements

From a quarantine project to a 5-year design review. Here is what rebuilding my room taught me about design, space utilization, and the importance of personalizing your environment.

INTRODUCTION

Project Overview

In 2021, during the COVID lockdown in Bangkok, I gave myself a challenge: model a full renovation of my room in Autodesk 3ds Max. No brief, no client. Just online classes and a goal to stay sharp. What I didn't expect was how much designing a tiny space would change how I think about space itself. Scale, material depth — every decision in a small room carries far more weight than I realized.

This case study explores the journey of transforming a simple room into a functional and aesthetically pleasing space, highlighting the challenges faced, the design decisions made, and the lessons learned over five years of living in and evolving the space. The project encompasses 3D modeling, room layout planning, interior design considerations, and real-life measurements to create a cohesive and personalized environment.

Before Version of The Room

The original room presented several challenges that required thoughtful redesign and reorganization. The space felt cramped and poorly utilized, with furniture arranged inefficiently and limited storage solutions. The layout lacked a clear functional flow, making it difficult to move around comfortably and limiting the room's versatility for work, relaxation, and personal activities. The aesthetic was uninspiring, with minimal personalization and no cohesive design scheme. These limitations served as the catalyst for a comprehensive renovation project aimed at transforming the space into an organized, functional, and visually appealing environment.

2021 Version of the Room

The 2021 version of the room showed clear improvement compared to the earlier layout, but it was still not fully realistic or refined. At that time, I was still inexperienced, and my understanding of design, space planning, and practical living needs was limited. My knowledge was still developing, and my everyday experience of how a room should truly function was much less mature than it is in the 2026 version. Even so, this stage became an important foundation for learning what worked, what felt incomplete, and what needed to be improved.

2026 Version of the Room

The 2026 version of the room reflects a major improvement from the 2021 version. By that time, I had developed a clearer understanding of design, space planning, and how a room should function in real life. The layout became more realistic, more practical, and better suited to daily living. The room felt more organized, more comfortable, and more personal, showing how experience and growing design awareness can transform a space into something far more refined and useful.
